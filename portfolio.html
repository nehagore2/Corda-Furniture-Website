OUR DESIGN PORTFOLIO

Portfolio.

A collection of furniture ideas, natural materials, and thoughtful interior design inspiration.

Explore the design language behind Corda and discover how furniture can bring character to everyday spaces.

Where ideas become furniture.

Every furniture design begins with an idea. Shape, material, comfort, and function work together to create a piece that feels natural in its surroundings.

Our portfolio reflects an appreciation for clean forms, natural textures, and the small details that contribute to a thoughtful furniture design.

01. Natural Furniture Collection

Inspired by the warmth of wood.

This collection explores the visual character of wooden furniture and its ability to bring warmth into living spaces.

The design approach focuses on simple forms, natural textures, and furniture that complements different interior styles.

Design focus: Natural materials, balanced shapes, and everyday functionality.

02. Living Space Collection

Furniture that connects with interiors.

A thoughtfully designed interior brings together furniture, materials, and space. This concept explores how wooden furniture can contribute to a comfortable and welcoming environment.

The focus is on creating visual harmony between furniture and the surrounding interior.

Design focus: Interior balance, comfort, and natural styling.

03. Craftsmanship Details

The beauty of the making process.

Craftsmanship brings a sense of individuality to furniture. This project focuses on the details involved in creating a furniture design, from the initial idea to the finished appearance.

Material selection, construction, finishing, and attention to detail all contribute to the identity of a furniture piece.

Design focus: Precision, materials, and thoughtful finishing.

04. Minimalist Furniture Study

Less decoration, more purpose.

Minimalist furniture focuses on essential forms and practical design. This concept explores the relationship between simplicity, proportion, and usability.

Clean lines and carefully considered details can create furniture that feels refined without unnecessary visual complexity.

Design focus: Simplicity, functionality, and visual balance.

Materials that inspire our work.

Natural materials offer a variety of textures, tones, and visual qualities. Wood is especially valued for its warmth and distinctive character.

Our design inspiration comes from understanding how materials can complement furniture forms and contribute to the atmosphere of a room.

Our design process.

01. Inspiration
We explore furniture forms, natural materials, and interior styles to develop new design ideas.

02. Concept
The design direction is developed by considering proportions, materials, function, and visual appearance.

03. Details
Attention is given to the elements that bring a furniture concept together, including surfaces, shapes, and finishing.

04. Final Design
The completed concept brings together the design direction, material inspiration, and intended everyday use.
